Welcome aboard

Weatherlight Games & Collectibles is a game store in the centre of Évora, open since 2020. We run around 15 events a week for Magic: The Gathering, Pokémon TCG, One Piece Card Game, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Riftbound, Digimon, Gundam and Cyberpunk TCG, from relaxed weekly events to official tournaments.

Visiting Évora? You are welcome to join any event, and our whole team speaks English. English cards are allowed in most official tournaments in Portugal, and you can buy boosters, decks, sleeves and other accessories in store.

How to join

Most games use an official app for sign-ups, pairings and results, so create your account before you come. On the day, check in at our counter within the time shown in the event details. The entry fee is paid at check-in.

  • Magic: The GatheringMagic: The Gathering Companion app and a Wizards account (you join with the event code)
  • Yu-Gi-Oh!Neuron app and KONAMI ID (we can create one with you in store)
  • One Piece Card GameBandai TCG+ app and account
  • Riftbound: League of Legends TCGRiftbound Gaming Network account
  • Digimon Card GameBandai TCG+ app and account
  • Gundam Card GameBandai TCG+ app and account
  • Cyberpunk TCGMelee account
  • Pokémon TCGPokémon Player ID (we can create one with you in store); the Pokémon Access app is recommended

For Constructed events, bring your own deck. For Sealed, Draft and Prereleases, you get the cards at the event. Questions? Ask us on Discord.
